Case Overview

Amazing Facts wanted to rethink traditional Bible learning by making it feel as natural as talking to a trusted mentor. Instead of presenting lessons as static educational content, the product transforms every course into an interactive AI conversation that guides users through Scripture one question at a time.

The experience combines structured learning, conversational AI, and personal progress tracking into a single mobile application.

The Goal

Make Bible learning approachable for everyone Increase lesson completion rates Turn passive reading into active conversations Build long-term engagement through progress and personalization Create an experience that feels trustworthy, calm, and human

Challenge

Most Bible learning apps rely on long articles, videos, or linear courses that require high motivation and often feel overwhelming.

The challenge was to create an experience that:

Feels personal instead of educational Reduces friction for new users Keeps users engaged through conversation Preserves the authority of Scripture while introducing AI Creates habit-forming daily interactions

My Role

Lead Product Designer

Product Strategy UX Research User Flows Information Architecture Mobile UX/UI AI Conversation Design Design System Visual Direction Interactive Prototypes

Design Thinking

Rather than designing another educational app, I approached the product as a conversation platform.

The AI mentor became the primary interface, while lessons, progress, and onboarding naturally emerged inside the dialogue instead of feeling like separate product features.

This reduced cognitive load and made learning feel significantly more personal.

AI-First Experience

The biggest product decision was making AI the interface—not just a feature.

Users don’t browse complicated menus or search through large libraries. They simply start a conversation.

The mentor:

Recommends lessons Answers questions Explains biblical concepts Guides users through courses Remembers learning progress Creates a continuous relationship with the learner

Learning Experience

The educational system combines two familiar mental models:

Structured lesson progression Conversational interaction

Each lesson feels like an ongoing dialogue rather than a traditional course, making learning significantly more engaging while preserving clear progression and measurable milestones.

Visual Storytelling

Used photography as the primary storytelling element.

Rather than relying on lengthy descriptions, the page demonstrates expertise through real imagery, training environments, transformation-focused messaging, and an authentic personal presence.

Conversation UX

Designing AI interfaces is different from designing standard applications.

Special attention was given to:

Conversational pacing Message hierarchy Quick reply actions Contextual recommendations Reducing typing effort Maintaining natural interaction flow

Every screen was optimized to keep users inside the conversation rather than navigating complex interfaces.

Design System

Created a scalable mobile design system including:

Reusable conversation components Lesson cards Progress indicators AI message patterns Onboarding flows Authentication screens Typography system Accessibility-ready color palette

This allowed rapid iteration while maintaining visual consistency across the entire product.

Process

Product Discovery User Journey Mapping AI Conversation Architecture Information Architecture Wireframes & User Flows Visual Design Interactive Prototypes Design System Usability Refinement

Results

Delivered a complete mobile experience that successfully combines:

AI mentoring Structured Bible courses Progress tracking Personalized learning Conversational UX Scalable product architecture

The final product feels approachable for new believers while remaining valuable for experienced learners seeking a more engaging way to study Scripture.

What I Learned

Designing AI products isn’t only about creating chat interfaces.

The real challenge is knowing when the conversation should guide the user—and when the interface should quietly support it.

This project reinforced how thoughtful conversational UX can make complex educational experiences feel intuitive, personal, and genuinely engaging.
